valued at approximately USD 130 million. I served as a core working-level member
for the academic component of the program.

## What I did

- Contributed as a key working-level member to the academic module of the
  next-generation integrated information system.
- Bridged day-to-day operations with the modernization effort to keep critical
  academic services reliable throughout the transition.
