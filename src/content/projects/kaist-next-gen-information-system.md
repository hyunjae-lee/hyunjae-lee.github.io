## Overview

KAIST ran a multi-year, institution-wide program (approximately **USD 130M**) to replace its
aging administrative systems with a single next-generation integrated information system.
I was a core working-level member responsible for the **academic domain** — the Academic
Affairs and Graduation Management systems that underpin the entire student lifecycle.

## Role & responsibilities

- Owned academic-domain requirements end to end: gathering needs from stakeholders,
  translating them into functional specifications, and validating them against the delivered system.
- Coordinated with the systems integrator and internal teams to align the academic module with
  the broader platform — shared data models, identity, and cross-module workflows.
- Safeguarded **data integrity** through the migration of years of academic records from legacy
  systems, with reconciliation and validation at each stage.

## Technical highlights

- **Legacy-to-target migration:** mapped and migrated academic records between the legacy and
  next-generation schemas, verifying completeness and correctness before cutover.
- **Integration:** ensured the academic module interoperated cleanly with shared services
  (authentication/authorization, common master data) across the integrated platform.
- **Operational continuity:** kept mission-critical academic services — enrollment, grades,
  graduation processing — running reliably throughout the transition.

## Impact

- Delivered the academic component of a ~USD 130M institution-wide modernization.
- Recognized with a **Special Commendation from KAIST (2025)** for the build.
