## Overview

Internal tools had grown a tangle of broad, long-lived access. I led a move to a
zero-trust model so every request is authenticated, authorized, and least-privilege by default.

## What I did

- Inventoried access paths and replaced standing permissions with scoped, auditable grants.
- Rolled out multi-factor authentication for all internal services.
- Documented the model so new services onboard securely by default.

## Results

- Removed a large share of over-broad permissions.
- Made access reviews fast and evidence-based.
