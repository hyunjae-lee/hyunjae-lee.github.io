## Overview

Teams were finding out about outages from users instead of from their own systems.
I built a centralized observability pipeline so failures surface in seconds, not minutes.

## What I did

- Deployed a metrics + logging stack and standardized instrumentation across services.
- Defined actionable alerts with clear ownership and runbooks, reducing noise.
- Built dashboards that made system health legible to both engineers and stakeholders.

## Results

- Mean-time-to-detect for incidents dropped substantially.
- On-call load became predictable and far less noisy.
