## Overview

As an undergraduate research assistant in the Distributed Platforms & Security Lab at
Chung-Ang University, I worked on preventing double-spending attacks in private
blockchains. The work was published at IEEE and filed as a patent.

## Approach

- Proposed a **recipient-oriented transaction** model that avoids double-spending by
  controlling the timing of validation.
- Applied **stealth addresses** and **masternodes** on the recipient side to preserve
  transactional privacy and make recipients active participants rather than passive
  receivers of propagated transactions.

## Outputs

- **Paper:** *Recipient-Oriented Transaction for Preventing Double Spending Attacks in
  Private Blockchain*, **IEEE SECON 2018** (with M. Shin, K. S. Kim, Y. Kang, J. Kim) —
  [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8397151/).
- **Patent:** *Recipient-oriented transaction verification method and apparatus for
  preventing double spending* — **KR 10-2018-0089040** (2018).
